EP09 — The Night I Realized I Had to Keep Going
Going back to work was supposed to be a step forward.
But I soon realized that starting again does not mean everything suddenly becomes easier.
I went back to work because I needed to support myself.
I wanted to earn my own money again. I wanted to take responsibility for my life and continue building something that belonged to me.
But inside, I was still carrying many things.
My relationship was still complicated.
There were moments of love, moments of misunderstanding, and moments when jealousy and insecurity made everything more difficult.
At the same time, I was trying to work, take care of myself, and continue thinking about my future.
Sometimes, I felt like I was dealing with too many things at once.
I didn't always know who I could talk to.
From the outside, people may see someone going to work every day and think that everything is fine.
But they cannot see what that person carries inside.
There were nights when I felt completely exhausted.
Not only physically, but emotionally.
I kept asking myself whether I was making the right decisions.
Whether I was strong enough.
Whether I could really build the life I wanted.
One night, around three in the morning, everything felt especially heavy.
I was overwhelmed and not thinking clearly.
I felt like I needed to get out, even though I knew I wasn't in the right state of mind.
So I called the person I loved.
I needed to hear his voice.
I needed someone to help me stop for a moment and think.
But he didn't answer.
For about twenty minutes, I waited.
Those twenty minutes felt much longer than they really were.
Then he called me back.
He told me to go back, rest, and calm down.
It may have sounded simple, but that moment meant something to me.
It reminded me that sometimes, when we are overwhelmed, we don't need all the answers immediately.
Sometimes we just need to stop.
Breathe.
And make it through the night.
After that night, I thought about my life differently.
I realized that I could not wait for everything around me to become perfect before I continued living.
My relationship might still have problems.
My work might not be the job I dreamed about.
My future might still be uncertain.
But I still had things I wanted to build.
I still had dreams.
I still had responsibilities.
And I still had myself.
I also believe that God sees what people cannot see.
He sees the struggles we don't talk about.
He sees the sacrifices we make.
He sees the nights when we are trying our best just to keep going.
I don't know why some things have happened in my life.
I don't know what the future will bring.
But I believe that the things I have lost, the things I have learned, and the things I am going through now will become part of the woman I am becoming.
So I decided to keep going.
Not because I suddenly became strong.
Not because everything was solved.
But because I understood something important:
I cannot build my future by giving up on myself.
I have to work.
I have to learn.
I have to take care of myself.
I have to keep building my website.
I have to keep looking for opportunities.
And little by little, I have to create something that is truly mine.
Maybe starting over is not about having a perfect plan.
Maybe it is simply about choosing to take another step when you don't know exactly where the road will lead.
That night, I was tired.
I was confused.
I was hurting.
But I chose to continue.
And maybe that is what strength really is.
Not never falling apart.
But finding a reason to stand up again.
I am still here.
And I am still building the life I want.
