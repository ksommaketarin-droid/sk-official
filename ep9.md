EP09 — The Night I Realized I Had to Keep Going
Going back to work was a decision I knew I had to make.
But making that decision did not mean that everything suddenly became easier.
I was working again, but there were still many things happening in my life at the same time.
My relationship was still complicated.
There were still moments of jealousy, misunderstanding, and uncertainty.
Sometimes I wanted to explain everything clearly, but I didn't always know how.
I was also trying to take care of myself while thinking about my future.
I wanted to earn money.
I wanted to continue building my website.
I wanted to find opportunities to work online.
And I wanted to create something that could eventually become my own source of income.
But sometimes, when too many things happen at once, even a person who is trying to be strong can become tired.
There were moments when I felt alone.
Not because there was nobody around me, but because some feelings are difficult to explain to anyone.
I had chosen to come to India.
I had chosen to start a new life.
And because these were my choices, sometimes I felt that I had to carry the consequences by myself.
One night, everything became too heavy.
It was around three in the morning.
I was extremely stressed and was not thinking clearly.
I felt like I needed to leave for a while, even though I knew I was not in the right state of mind.
So I called my boyfriend.
I wanted to hear his voice.
I wanted someone I trusted to help me calm down.
But he did not answer.
I waited for about twenty minutes.
Those twenty minutes felt very long.
Then he called me back.
He told me to go back, rest, and calm down.
That moment made me stop and think.
Sometimes, when we are overwhelmed, we want to escape from everything.
But running away does not always solve what is hurting us.
Sometimes we simply need to stop, breathe, and give ourselves time to think again.
That night did not solve all my problems.
My relationship did not suddenly become perfect.
My work did not suddenly become easy.
And my future was still uncertain.
But something inside me changed.
I realized that I could not wait for everything in my life to become perfect before I continued moving forward.
I still had responsibilities.
I still had work to do.
I still had dreams.
And I still had a life to build.
I also believe that God sees things that other people cannot see.
He sees the things we keep inside.
He sees the sacrifices we make.
He sees the moments when we are tired but still choose to continue.
I don't know what the future will bring.
I don't know whether everything I hope for will happen exactly the way I imagine it.
But I know that I cannot give up on myself.
I have already come too far.
I left one life behind and came to another country to begin again.
I returned to work because I needed to support myself.
I started building my website because I wanted something of my own.
And now I understand that rebuilding a life takes time.
It is not one big decision.
It is many small decisions made every day.
Getting up.
Going to work.
Learning.
Trying again.
Making mistakes.
And continuing anyway.
That night, I was tired.
I was confused.
I was carrying more than I wanted to admit.
But I chose to keep going.
Maybe strength is not about never feeling weak.
Maybe strength is knowing that you are tired and still choosing not to give up on yourself.
I don't know exactly where this road will take me.
But I know what I want.
I want to stand on my own.
I want to build something that belongs to me.
And I want to create a future that I can be proud of.
I am still here.
And I am still building.
