India was a country I never imagined I would live in.

Coming to India was not simply a journey to another country. For me, it meant leaving behind the life I knew and stepping into a life that was completely unfamiliar.

Everything around me was different. The people, the language, the food, and the culture.

At first, even simple everyday things could feel difficult. I didn't know the language well, and I didn't always know how to communicate with people.

Food was one of the biggest challenges. The spices, the aromas, and the flavors were very different from the food I had grown up eating in Thailand.

There were times when I wondered whether I would really be able to live here.

But little by little, I learned.

I learned what to eat. I learned how to communicate. I learned how to move through everyday life in a country that was completely new to me.

The language was difficult, but I started learning by listening, remembering words, and trying to speak.

Every small thing I learned became a small victory.

Slowly, India began to feel less unfamiliar.

And while I was trying to rebuild my life, something unexpected happened.

I met someone through an app.

At that moment, I didn't know how important this person would become in my life.

I didn't know that meeting him would lead me into a story filled with love, happiness, pain, trust, and doubt.

I only knew that someone new had entered my life.

And from that moment, my journey in India was about to change.

The story of us was about to begin.
