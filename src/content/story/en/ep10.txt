EP10 — Standing on My Own Again
After I got back to my accommodation, I kept thinking about everything that had happened.
I knew I could not continue living like that.
I needed to work.
Not because I wanted to forget my problems. Not because of another person.
I needed to work because I had responsibilities that could not wait.
For about three months, I had stopped working and had been using the money my boyfriend was giving me to support myself.
I knew he had tried to help me as much as he could. But eventually, he had to take on bank debt too.
That was when I started asking myself:
How could I continue letting him carry everything for me?
The answer was simple.
I couldn't.
I still had problems in my relationship. I still had doubts, questions, and things that hurt me.
But my life still had to move forward.
So I made the decision to go back to work.
It was not an easy decision.
After everything I had been through, I had lost a lot of my confidence. Going back to work while my heart was still not ready made me feel pressured and confused.
But I knew there was at least one thing I could do.
I could start taking responsibility for my own life again.
I didn't know what would happen next.
I didn't know how my relationship would end.
But this time, I understood that I could not wait for someone else to solve my whole life for me.
I had to start with myself.
I needed to earn my own money. I needed to take care of myself. I needed to learn. And I needed to try to stand on my own again.
Maybe going back to work was not the end of my story.
Maybe it was the beginning of another chapter.
I was a woman who had once felt as if she had lost everything.
Now I was slowly learning that even when life does not turn out the way I hoped, even when my heart is still hurting, and even when I still have many unanswered questions,
I can still choose to get back up.
I can still choose to build my life again.
